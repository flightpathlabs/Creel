# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Stranglekelp counts as a normal catch, since it comes up on the line
  often on Forever. It's in the Collection, with what it's used for,
  instead of arriving as a unique find.
- Trip records count the time after a trip's last catch. The longest trip
  could show a little short of the trip itself.
- The Next page drops a suggested recipe as soon as you learn it.
- A recipe card's skill line no longer runs past the card's edge, and a
  card left open no longer comes back by itself when the book reopens.
- Demo mode: the Next page no longer asks a low-level character for a
  level, and a marked place or map pin doesn't carry over between the
  demo and your own book.

## 0.1.0 (14 November 2026)

First version.

- A setup page on first login: how you cast, when the panel shows, and a
  few finishing touches. Nothing is bound until you finish it.
- One key to cast and reel, or a double right-click to cast. The key goes
  back to its own job in combat.
- The panel: skill, a status line, the lure slot, a line timer, time this
  trip and in all, whether fish get away at this spot, the catch card with
  vendor and auction values and a tip, the trip's tally and worth.
- Quiet while fishing, with your own sound settings always put back.
- The Fishing Book: Collection, Places, Trips, Earnings and Next.
- Next: where your next rank comes from, the nearest trainer, where to fish
  at your skill and what's worth doing now, with map pins.
- Recipe cards: how to make anything a catch turns into, what it costs and
  whether it's worth it, with a link into Goldwise's crafting tree.
- Unique finds: catches Creel doesn't know about get their own section, a
  grand moment with the epic-loot sound, and a report to copy for the
  issue form. Valuable catches play the auction house's sound.
- Past catch spots on the world map, if you turn it on.
- A line on fish tooltips: how many you've caught and where best.
- A minimap button.
- A demo mode for screenshots: `/creel demo`.
